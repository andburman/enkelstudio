The service

myeconomy is a private personal finance tool built by Enkel Studio (Anders Burman, Sweden). It is used only by its operator and household to view their own bank accounts and keep a budget. It is not offered to the public, and no other users can register.

Bank access

Account information is retrieved through Enable Banking Oy, read-only, after the account holder authenticates with their bank. The tool cannot initiate payments or change anything at the bank. Access may be revoked at any time.

No warranty

The tool is provided as is. Balances and budgets shown are for personal overview only; the bank's own records are always authoritative.
