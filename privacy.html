Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-22

What this covers

This policy covers myeconomy, a personal finance tool built and operated by Enkel Studio (Anders Burman, Sweden). It is a private, non-commercial tool used only by its operator and household. It is not offered to the public and does not accept sign-ups.

Data processed

With the account holder's explicit consent, given through bank authentication (BankID), the tool retrieves account information from the account holder's own banks via Enable Banking Oy: account names, balances and transactions (date, amount, counterparty and description).

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used solely to give the account holder an overview of their own finances and budget.
  • Data is stored on the operator's own encrypted computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for marketing.
  • Analysis may be performed with an AI assistant at the account holder's own request. Account numbers are not shared with it.

Consent and retention

Bank access is valid for at most 180 days and can be revoked at any time through the bank or by contacting us. Stored data is kept for as long as the account holder uses the tool and is deleted on request.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your data. You may also lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).
